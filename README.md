# SUNVOLT Technologies website starter
## Included
- Responsive one-page website (`index.html`)
- Sections for services, approach, customer segments and contact
- Mobile navigation
- Enquiry form UI (demonstration mode; not connected to an inbox or CRM)

## Before publishing
1. Confirm the legal company name and whether incorporation is complete.
2. Add approved phone number, email address, physical/service-area details and social links.
3. Connect the enquiry form to an approved backend, CRM or form service. The current form does not transmit submissions.
4. Add genuine project photographs, team details, customer testimonials and verified credentials only after obtaining permission/evidence.
5. Confirm all service claims and licensing arrangements. Do not imply licences or certifications that SUNVOLT does not hold.
6. Add privacy notice and cookie controls if required by the final analytics/forms implementation.
7. Test on mobile, desktop and major browsers before connecting the domain.

## Preview
Open `index.html` in a modern browser.

## Deployment
Upload `index.html` to the web root of your chosen hosting provider, or deploy the folder as a static site. Point `sunvolt.tech` DNS records to the hosting provider after hosting is configured.
